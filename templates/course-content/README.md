# Statutory pages, copied into every new course

An Impressum under § 5 DDG and a Datenschutzerklärung under Art. 13 DSGVO are
not optional for a site published from Germany, and a course that goes live
without them is the one real legal exposure in this whole programme.

They carry the author's actual name, address and contact — the same author for
every course in this organisation — so they are inherited rather than
scaffolded as ⟨PLACEHOLDER⟩ blanks. A blank that must be filled before
publishing is a blank that gets published: the kit's own example still carries
its ⟨…⟩ and needs a declared exception to stop gate A5 failing on it.

What IS substituted per course is `{{CONTENT_LICENCE}}`, from boulingua.yml.
Four of the eighteen languages ship transcript-only audio because their source
material's licence differs, so a default here would be copied without being
chosen.

Updating an address means updating every course. That is a real cost and it is
smaller than the alternative, which is a site published with a placeholder
where its statutory notice should be.
